About this prototype

Goal: Build a life with more choices. Education, useful skills, reliable income, healthy relationships, assets, manageable debt and wellbeing all matter. There is no single millionaire win condition.

How time works: Each choice represents roughly three months. Salary, business and land income are paid automatically each quarter, followed by food, housing, transport and debt costs.

Important: This is a fictional game inspired by Zambian education, work, informal trade, mobile money and entrepreneurship. Values are simplified and should not be treated as official wages, school fees or business forecasts.

  • Education opens careers and improves your ability to grow a business.
  • Network and reputation unlock opportunities.
  • Food, housing and transport choices change your quarterly costs, energy and stress.
  • Some offers are deliberately too good to be true. Verify jobs, training, land and investments before paying.
  • Vehicles, land, savings and business value can create options, but debt and upkeep can reduce them.
  • Bank loans require enough trust, income and repayment capacity. High-cost informal debt is easier to obtain but much harder to escape.
  • A small hustle is uncertain. Most early attempts bring small or negative results; records, skills, customers and persistence improve the odds.
  • Low energy and high stress can cause setbacks.
  • Your game saves locally on the device when you press Save Game.
